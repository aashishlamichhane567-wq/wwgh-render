# "Look at the Lilies" (faith reel, ~18 s, storybook dialogue). Scene key = speaker + turn: b = the young man, j = Jesus.
# One line = one TTS take. Paraphrases Matthew 6:28-29 and 6:34 in our own words.

## b1
What if everything falls apart tomorrow?

## j1
Look at these lilies.

## b2
...Flowers?

## j2
They don't work. They don't worry.
And still, not even a king ever dressed like one of them.

## b3
So what am I supposed to do?

## j3
Let tomorrow worry about itself.
Today, I've got you.
