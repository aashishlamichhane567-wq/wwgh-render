# "Why Nepal's Hills Keep Falling" (landslide Short, ~35 s). One "## key" per scene, one line = one TTS take, blank line = longer pause.

## hook
In 2020{twenty twenty}, landslides killed 334{three hundred and thirty-four} people in Nepal.
A record.

## roads
The Himalaya was always steep and fragile. What changed... is us.
In thirty years, Nepal cut almost 60,000{sixty thousand} kilometres of rural roads.
Many by bulldozer. Most with no drainage.
In one district, those roads doubled the number of landslides.

## quake
Then the 2015{twenty fifteen} earthquake cracked the slopes open.
Now every monsoon hits loosened ground.

## end
The mountains didn't get weaker. We just keep cutting into them.
