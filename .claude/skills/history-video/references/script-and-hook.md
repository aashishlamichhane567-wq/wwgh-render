# Script and hook

## Format (episodes/<slug>/script.txt)

```
## hook
In 862{eight sixty-two}, a group of tribes in the north looked around and said:
"Our land is great and rich, but there is no order in it. Come and rule over us."
They said this to Vikings.
```

- `## key` starts a scene. Aim for 15–20 scenes, one per era or turning point, each 20–50 s.
- One line is one TTS take. Keep lines to 25 words or fewer, since long lines cost memory and drift.
- A blank line inside a scene gives a longer pause (0.9 s instead of 0.42 s).
- `shown{spoken}`: the caption shows `shown` while the narrator says `spoken`. Use it for years and figures.

## What the niche rewards

These are the habits of faceless animated-history channels (e.g. the "entire history of X" format):

- **Hook in 20–25 s**, in this order:
  1. a strange true quote or fact;
  2. a punchline that reframes it ("They said this to Vikings.");
  3. the scale ("biggest country on Earth, eleven time zones");
  4. the promise ("This is the entire history of Russia. Let's go.").
- **A joke or absurd true detail every 20–30 s.** Deadpan works best: "officially of haemorrhoidal colic", "a
  Pizza Hut commercial".
- **Running gags with callbacks.** For example "the second ruler in this video to kill his own son. Keep count."
- **Short sentences, concrete numbers, named people.** Explain jargon in five words or fewer.
- **Ending:** a rapid recap list that echoes the hook, a full-circle callback to the opening quote, then one CTA
  ("Which country should we do next?").
- Dark history is stated plainly and gravely, never joked about: famines, purges, wars.

## Accuracy

Round figures and say "about". Use the commonly cited estimates. Mark legends as legends ("Legend says...", "The autopsy
says it was mostly the bullets."). For contested modern events, use measured, factual wording.
